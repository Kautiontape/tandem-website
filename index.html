Tandem Alarm

An alarm clock for two phones.

About

Tandem Alarm pairs two phones with a 6-character code. Each phone keeps its own alarms and rings them itself, with no network needed, and each person can see the other’s alarms. From the paired phone you can skip the next ring, turn an alarm off or on, or stop one that’s ringing.

Join an alarm on the other phone and both phones ring together, with its photo and sound. Snooze or stop on either phone, and the other follows. No account and no ads.

Get Tandem Alarm

Tandem Alarm is an Android app, currently in closed testing on Google Play. You can also download the APK from GitHub.
